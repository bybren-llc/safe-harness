## When to use

## Shape

- What we found (bullets, each with a source)
- What is `UNKNOWN`
- Recommendation (optional, ranked) and who should decide
- Privacy class of any cited store (concept name only)

## Sources

Prefer in-repo docs and allowlisted public URLs. If a source is official but not on the allowlist, name it and leave the URL `UNKNOWN` until the HITL owner allowlists it.

## Stop rules

- Do not invent links, quotes, or product claims.
- Do not treat the brief as a decision. See the cited section; this skill grants no authority.
