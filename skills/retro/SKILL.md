## When to use

A bolt or wave ended. Measure against the value set named at the start. The template example set is M1–M6 in the cited `constitution/00` section. Adopters may use their own names; do not invent metrics after the fact.

## Shape

- What we said we would measure
- What happened (evidence, not vibe)
- Worked / didn't
- Keep / adapt / drop (cited `constitution/06`)
- Follow-up issues

Be specific. A retro without a keep/adapt/drop line is a diary entry.

## Empiricism

Techniques that win in a controlled comparison replace prior defaults. See the cited section; this skill grants no authority.

## Stop rules

- No public or external posts without the HITL owner's explicit OK.
- Do not silently change constitution defaults from a retro note.
